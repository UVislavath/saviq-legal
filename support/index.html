SaviQ Support

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Email saviq.support@gmail.com. Never attach private documents, passwords or screenshots of private content.

For help, use Email SaviQ Support under Settings > Help & Support. The email subject includes your SaviQ version and build; add your iOS version and a short description of what happened.

Never attach private documents, screenshots of private content, credentials, passwords, OCR text, authentication tokens, or other vault information to a support email. Replace personal examples with synthetic information.

Common help

  • Camera or photo access: Open iPhone Settings, choose SaviQ, and review the applicable permission.
  • Sign-in trouble: Check your internet connection and retry. Your local vault remains available while signed out.
  • Import trouble: Confirm the PDF or common image file remains available on the device, then retry.
  • App Lock trouble: Use the device-passcode option supplied by iOS. Authentication failure never deletes the local vault.

SaviQ Version 1 does not provide cloud document recovery. Apple device backup may contain eligible app data when enabled, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior.
